AY2025 Activity Report

Important

This content was automatically translated by an LLM (2026/10/05). The original content is written in Japanese.

The following was written on Monday, October 27, 2025.

  • As of April 1, I joined the Architecture, Civil and Environmental Engineering Program, Faculty of Engineering and Design, Kagawa University as an Associate Professor.
  • Two fourth-year students, Ago and Abe, joined the lab. Looking back, my first lab at NIT, Akita College, sixteen years ago also started with two students.

The following was written all at once on 2026/10/02.

  • 2025/4/23 We held a lab kickoff party.
  • 2025/5/28 I attended a working group of the consortium on advancing simulation technologies for the Human-Mobility-Society, and tried the MR pedestrian simulator developed by Setouchi Simulator Co., Ltd.
  • 2025/6/7-2025/6/8 The 71st JSCE Spring Conference on Infrastructure Planning and Management was held at Kagawa University’s Saiwai-cho Campus. Between helping with its operation, I was able to greet several long-time colleagues on my new appointment.
  • 2025/8/6-2025/8/8 I attended the JSTE Conference on Traffic Engineering and presented “Effects of Vehicle Speed and Pedestrian-Vehicle Distance on Anxiety on Residential Streets” (in Japanese), based on the graduation research of a former student (Komatsu) from my time at NIT, Akita College.
  • 2025/9/26 The interim presentation of graduation research was held, and Ago and Abe gave presentations.
  • 2025/9/29 I attended the Tokushima University Graduate School of Technology, Industrial and Social Sciences Research Exchange Meeting 2025 and presented “A Trial of Traffic Safety Classes for Preschool and Elementary School Children Using Generative AI and Augmented Reality” (in Japanese), based on the graduation research of former students (Sugawara and Onuki) from NIT, Akita College.
  • Our project “Non-verbal Communication between Pedestrians and Drivers at Unsignalized Crosswalks through Field Observation and Distributed Simulation” was awarded research funding under the FY2025 HMS Consortium Participating Researcher Support Program of the Kagawa University Institute for Innovation Design.
  • 2025/10/22 Four third-year undergraduate students, Ogawa, Saeki, Yasuba, and Yamasaki, joined the lab.
  • 2025/10/25 At the “Aioi Nissay Dowa Insurance SAFETOWNDRIVE” held at Yashima REXAM Field, I gave a lecture in “Part 1: Traffic Safety Class” together with officers from the Kagawa Prefectural Police. Mr. Matsumoto, captain of the Japan national deaf football team, led “Part 2: What Can Be Conveyed in a World without Sound” and “Part 3: Deaf Football Experience,” and I was impressed by the agile movements of an active athlete.
  • 2025/11/12 We held a lab kickoff party (the second this year) to welcome the newly assigned third-year students.
  • 2025/12/12 We held a joint year-end seminar with the Tamaki Lab and Suzuki Lab, where Ago and Abe gave presentations. Before the seminar, we took group photos for the graduation album, and afterwards a joint year-end party was held with alumni of both labs.
  • 2026/1/8 We held a joint seminar at Kagawa University’s Hayashi-cho Campus with Prof. Nakanishi (University of Canberra), Prof. Sugiura and D1 student Sase (Kochi University of Technology), and Hasegawa and Abe from Kagawa University. After the seminar, we moved to Kawaramachi and enjoyed good wine and food together.
  • 2026/1/16 Our paper “Relationship between Pedestrians’ Anxiety and Vehicle Speed and Pedestrian-Vehicle Distance on Residential Streets” (in Japanese), a refined version of the August presentation submitted to the Journal of JSTE (special issue), was accepted.
  • 2026/1/30 At the HMS Consortium meeting at Aichi Shukutoku University, I tried the bicycle simulator and the small-mobility simulator owned by Prof. Mori of the university. Both were full-scale systems and very inspiring.
  • 2026/2/20 The graduation thesis presentation was held, and Ago and Abe presented the results of their year of work. At the meeting after the presentations, both were approved for their graduation research credits.
  • 2026/2/24 We held a farewell party for the lab. That said, five of the six students will remain in the lab next year. The year passed in the blink of an eye.
