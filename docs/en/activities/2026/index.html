AY2026 Activity Report

Important

This content was automatically translated by an LLM (2026/10/05). The original content is written in Japanese.

  • 2026/4/8 Gu Jiaan from China joined us as a research student. With four undergraduates, one first-year master’s student, and one faculty member, we start our second year at Kagawa University as a team of seven.
  • 2026/4/13 We held a welcome party for Gu, which also served as the lab kickoff party.
  • 2026/5/29 I attended the JSAE 2026 Annual Congress (Spring) at Pacifico Yokohama North and presented “Comparison of Pedestrian Behaviors Observed in Real-World Surveys and MR Pedestrian Simulator Experiments” (in Japanese), based on Ago’s graduation research. Since most participants are from the automotive industry, including manufacturers, the differences from the conferences I usually attend were interesting. I was also surprised by the number of participants and the size of the venue.
  • 2026/8/4-2026/8/6 I attended the JSTE Conference on Traffic Engineering, where I was scheduled to present “Effects of Traffic Conditions on Drivers’ Stopping Behavior at Unsignalized Crosswalks” (in Japanese), based on the graduation research of a former student (Abe) from NIT, Akita College. However, since August 4 (Tue) was the day of final exams for two of my courses, I asked my co-author, Prof. Kasai, to present on my behalf. I hurried to Tokyo after the exams but could not make it on the first day, so I joined in a relaxed manner from the second day.
  • From after the Obon holidays until mid-September, I was busy with my KAKENHI grant application. It was my first time receiving an external review, which was very helpful; on the other hand, I (as always) got started late, and I deeply regret not scheduling enough time to receive multiple reviews.
  • 2026/8/25 Hasegawa and Ogawa joined the HMS Consortium WG2 and WG4 meeting held at the Kagawa University Institute for Innovation Design online, and Ogawa presented the progress of the graduation research.
  • 2026/9/22 We were notified that our paper “A Pedestrian Behavior Pattern Description Method for Comparing Real-world Observations and MR Simulator Experiments” (in Japanese), based on the research presented at JSAE in May, was accepted for publication in the Transactions of JSAE. It was my first submission to the journal, and I am relieved that it was accepted in a little over three months from submission (even though it went through a third round of review).
  • 2026/9/24 We participated in the first AY2026 meeting of the Research Institute for Safe and Secure Social Systems, Tohoku Institute of Technology, held in Sendai City, where Hasegawa (in person) and Yamasaki (online) gave presentations.
  • 2026/9/29 The interim presentation of graduation research was held, and four students, Ogawa, Saeki, Yasuba, and Yamasaki, gave presentations. I think each of them presented well.
